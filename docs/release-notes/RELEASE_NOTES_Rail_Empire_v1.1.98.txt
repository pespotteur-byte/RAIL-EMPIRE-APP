Rail Empire v1.1.98 — LIVEMAP SIV AUDIO UNMUTE / WAV COMPATIBILITY
==================================================================
Base: v1.1.97 STOP CURSOR + SIV FRENCH FIX.

Terrain feedback fixed
----------------------
The v1.1.97 French-only guard was placed too early: if Chromium/Opera exposed no
French SpeechSynthesis voice at the exact click instant, announce() returned
before even playing the authentic RER-B fixed recording. The result was total
silence.

Changes
-------
1. Authentic RER-B audio no longer depends on SpeechSynthesis readiness.
   - The recorded "Attention" starts immediately on an explicit train click.
   - Chromium/SAPI French voices are resolved in parallel.
   - Missing or delayed voice enumeration can no longer mute recorded audio.

2. Delayed Chromium voice discovery handled.
   - The announcer waits/polls briefly for a real fr/fr-FR voice because old
     Chromium/Windows builds may expose an empty getVoices() list initially.
   - English/default voices remain forbidden for station names.

3. Old-PC codec compatibility.
   - The three original RER-B fixed snippets are now also bundled as PCM WAV.
   - WAV is attempted first, original M4A/AAC remains as fallback.
   - This avoids depending on AAC decoder availability on old Windows installs.

4. Failure behaviour is no longer all-or-nothing.
   - If no French variable-name voice genuinely exists, the real recorded
     "Attention" is still audible and a diagnostic is kept instead of silently
     cancelling the whole announcement.
   - RE never selects an English voice for French station names.

Preserved
---------
- v1.1.97 stop-cursor/save self-heal.
- v1.1.96 early-arrival station detection and clean Livemap route rendering.
- Original RER-B snippets remain the fixed announcement wording.
- No catalogue changes.
