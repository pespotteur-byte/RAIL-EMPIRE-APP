Rail Empire v1.1.99 — HOTFIX13
Canton reservation recovery / [BLOQUE] departure fix

This hotfix targets a confirmed departure deadlock where a train could have valid departure prerequisites but stay at 0 km/h with [BLOQUE] on Livemap.

The defect was inside the canton interlocking reservation path. reserveNextAhead() calls CantonManager.reserve(); unlike occupy() and isAvailable(), reserve() did not evict a dead owner before returning false. A failed blocked_route service could also remain visible and therefore was not considered dead by the canton manager.

HOTFIX13 makes the interlocking ownership rules consistent:
- stale direct canton reservations are removed;
- stale canton occupations are removed;
- blocked_route is terminal for interlocking ownership;
- live train reservations are never stolen.

FILE cache is bumped to 1199dep5 so browsers do not reuse HOTFIX12.
