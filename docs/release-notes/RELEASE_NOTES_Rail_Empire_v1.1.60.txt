Rail Empire v1.1.60 — Infogare Exact Photo + Live Timetable Fix
Date: 2026-08-17

PURPOSE
Correct the v1.1.58/v1.1.59 Infogare remaster after field testing exposed two regressions:
1) supplied reference photographs were being visually covered by large recreated opaque backgrounds;
2) Infogare train lists were sourced mainly from already-spawned runtime services, so future V2 timetable trains could be absent.

FIXES
- The user-supplied Infogare images are now the authoritative visible boards.
- RER RATP, RER SNCF/Transilien and DB use the exact original supplied photographs directly.
- AFL DP, AFL AR, CATI 3-3, CATI AR, CATI COMPLET, Ecran Quai, Flash Circulation and Palette retain the exact supplied flat images.
- Removed obsolete cropped/redrawn RATP, Transilien and DB derivative assets.
- Removed the large opaque board/row repaint masks from the problematic photo renderers.
- Only small changing text regions are masked/animated over the original photographs.
- Ecran Quai, Palette, RATP, RER SNCF and DB therefore keep the original photographic visual character.
- Palette keeps the photographed analogue clock and overlays live clock hands.
- DB keeps the original angled photograph and receives a lightweight live-data overlay rather than a recreated rectangular board.

LIVE TIMETABLE DATA
- Infogare now reads Schedule Creator V2 timetable plans / rotations directly, before a service has spawned on the Livemap.
- Reads J-1, J and J+1 planning windows to correctly handle after-midnight services and upcoming trains.
- Supports rotation-assigned and direct-assignment V2 schedules.
- Runtime services are used as live overlays for current delay/state/cancellation where available, rather than as the sole source of trains.
- Planned and spawned representations are deduplicated.
- Arrival and departure times are handled independently at intermediate stations.
- Legacy service compatibility is retained.

STATION SEARCH
- Keeps the v1.1.59 station autocomplete: direct typing, accent/punctuation-insensitive matching, max 12 suggestions, gameplay station ID selection.

UNCHANGED
- OSM/ORM routing logic from v1.1.57 is untouched.
- Schedule routing, route memory, spatial OSM cache, Livemap runtime and rolling-stock logic are not redesigned by this release.
