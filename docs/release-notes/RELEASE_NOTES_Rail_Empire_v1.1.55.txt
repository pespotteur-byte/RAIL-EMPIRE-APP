Rail Empire v1.1.55 — LIVEMAP ORM-FOLLOWING TRAIN MARKERS
===========================================================

Livemap train marker redesign
-----------------------------
- Replaces category PNG/pictogram train icons with a vector-only marker:
  solid coloured dot + small directional arrowhead.
- No centre pictogram.
- No permanent moving-train glow. The selected train keeps a light selection ring.
- Marker size adapts to Livemap zoom.

Seven explicit train categories / colours
-----------------------------------------
- Voyageur: blue  #3b82f6
- Fret:      green #22c55e
- W:         grey  #94a3b8
- HLP:       dark grey #334155
- TM:        red   #ef4444
- Infra:     yellow #facc15
- TTX:       orange #f97316

TTX is now a real Schedule Creator V2 category, distinct from Infra.
Legacy 'work' services are normalized to TTX for old-save display compatibility.
New TTX means generic train de travaux. Historical legacy 'work' keeps its old
catenary-work compatibility rule; new TTX follows the actual traction/electrical
compatibility of the assigned material.

ORM/OSM route following
-----------------------
- The marker heading is derived from the CURRENT routed OSM/ORM polyline segment.
- It no longer points merely toward the next station.
- Curves, S-curves and turnout branches therefore rotate the arrow according to
  the actual route used by the running train.
- Rotation uses shortest-angle smoothing (~80 ms visual time constant) to avoid
  visible snapping at route vertices without changing physical train movement.

Performance
-----------
- Normal detailed marker rendering remains active through 1,000 simultaneous trains.
- Above 1,000 services, circles + arrowheads are batched by the seven colours,
  preserving direction/category while avoiding thousands of independent image draws.
- Removed train-marker PNG loading from the Livemap renderer.
- FILE:// split bundle architecture remains unchanged: 61 core modules; 66 deferred
  catalogue modules.
