Rail Empire v1.1.61 — RER RATP LIVE BOARD
==========================================

Scope
-----
RER RATP Infogare only. Other Infogare models and the OSM/ORM routing engine are unchanged.

Implemented from the player-validated mock/specification
--------------------------------------------------------
- Player company name replaces the fixed RER A logo block (example: PEET).
- Current time remains at the upper right.
- Header shows the two most frequent final destinations among departures from the selected station over the next 24 h.
- Five fixed departure rows.
- Left badge = train name/code.
- Main row text = final destination.
- Waiting time:
  * 0–59 minutes: number with small "min" underneath.
  * 60 minutes and above: h:mm (example 1:07), with no "h:min" legend.
- A train remains visible at 0 min for one minute after its planned/live departure time, then the list shifts.
- Incident footer is dynamic and only includes active incidents touching the selected station, stations served by departures from it, or a directly relevant live service.
- Future V2 timetable rows keep served station IDs so incident filtering does not depend on a train already being spawned.
- RER RATP has no paging: it always shows the next five eligible departures.

Typography
----------
The renderer prefers the CSS font family "Parisine". No Parisine font file is bundled; when unavailable on the player's system/browser, Rail Empire falls back to Arial Narrow / Segoe UI / Arial.
