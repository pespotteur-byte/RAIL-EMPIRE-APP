=================================================

FIELD FIX
---------
The Schedule Creator V2 no longer requires an exact OSM/ORM way to be discovered around the gameplay station icon.

New station workflow:
  1. Click the station icon.
  2. Rail Empire enters track-position selection mode.
  3. Click the exact desired position on the visible railway track.
  4. Enter the mandatory Livemap track name.

The cursor position is now the authoritative station-track anchor. This avoids failures caused by gameplay station icons being offset from the physical railway or by OSM ways being split/renumbered.

VIA and technical-point anchors also use direct cursor positions. The real route is then calculated on the ORM/OSM railway graph.

ABSOLUTE RULE RETAINED
----------------------
No synthetic straight-line route is accepted in Schedule Creator V2.
If the real ORM graph cannot connect the selected cursor anchors, the route is rejected with an error and no diagonal/fake line is drawn.
